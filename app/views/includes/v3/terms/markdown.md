You must read and agree to these terms and conditions for the responsible use of HMPPS Digital Services. 

The terms and conditions govern your user account and access. They apply to all users including: 

-   Ministry of Justice (MOJ) employees, contractors, consultants, and temporary staff 
-   staff from other agencies, such as the Police 

HMPPS Digital Services contain sensitive information. By using HMPPS Digital Services, you confirm you understand and agree to the following things. 

## What are HMPPS Digital Services 

HMPPS Digital Services are: 

-   Digital Prison Services (DPS) 
-   Probation Digital Services (PDS) 

## Who we are 

Justice Digital manages HMPPS Digital Services on behalf of the Crown. Justice Digital is part of the Ministry of Justice and will be referred to as 'we' from now on. 

## Only access information you need for your job 

Only search, access, view, use or share prisoner or a person on probation's information when you have a legitimate work reason to do so. 

Having access to a person's prison or probation record does not mean you are authorised to view it. 

Accessing information without a legitimate work-related reason is unauthorised access. 

You must not: 

-   browse or search records out of curiosity, including friends or family 
-   look up prisoners or people on probation unless you need to as part of your role 
-   access records at another establishment or information for someone else, unless you are authorised to do so as part of your work 

Anything you do in HMPPS Digital Services must comply with the [Computer Misuse Act and other relevant data legislation](https://www.cps.gov.uk/prosecution-guidance/computer-misuse-act). 

MOJ employees, contractors, consultants, and temporary staff must read [the Acceptable use of IT policy.](https://justiceuk.sharepoint.com/sites/SecurityPolicyandGuidance/SitePages/Policy%20and%20Guidance/Asset%20Management/Acceptable-Use-Policy.aspx) 

## Keep information secure and confidential 

Information in HMPPS Digital Services is sensitive and must be handled responsibly. 

You must: 

-   keep prisoner, probation, and staff information confidential 
-   only share information with people inside or outside HMPPS who are authorised to receive it 
-   only print, copy, photograph, download or otherwise share information when you have a legitimate work reason and are authorised to do so 

MOJ employees, contractors, consultants, and temporary staff must follow [HMPPS policies when storing, sending or handling information](https://justiceuk.sharepoint.com/sites/SecurityPolicyandGuidance/SitePages/Policy%20and%20Guidance/Asset%20Management/Data-Handling-and-Information-Sharing-Guide.aspx?web=1) 

You must not: 

-   allow unauthorised people to view or use HMPPS Digital Services or your staff or work computer 
-   allow prisoners or people on probation to view screens, even those that contain their own information 
-   take screenshots or use web capture tools to take images from HMPPS Digital Services 

## Protect your account 

Your account is for your use only. You are accountable for what you do on your account, and for what someone else does if they use your account. Everything you do in HMPPS Digital Services is linked to you. 

Always keep your account details secure. You must never: 

-   share your username or password 
-   use someone else's account 
-   allow someone else to use your account 
-   leave your computer unlocked if unattended 

MOJ employees, contractors, consultants and agency staff must not access HMPPS Digital Services from a non-MOJ approved device. 

## How to report a security breach 

To report a security issue, go to [how to report security breaches](https://justiceuk.sharepoint.com/sites/HMPPSIntranet/SitePages/Information-security--report-a-data-loss(1).aspx?web=1&or=WORD-WEB.BODY.NT&ct=1791894596355&ct=1791896973781) on the intranet or telephone 020 3334 4502. 

## Keep records accurate and use HMPPS Digital Services professionally 

What you record is used across HMPPS to make operational decisions. Inaccurate entries can lead to poor decisions. 

You must: 

-   record information accurately 
-   update records promptly 
-   correct mistakes where appropriate 
-   only use HMPPS Digital Services for official HMPPS business 

All data you enter into HMPPS Digital Services is potentially liable to legal disclosure under the [Data Protection Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents) and/or the [Freedom of Information Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36/contents). 

## Your activity is recorded and may be monitored 

HMPPS Digital Services keeps an audit trail of your activity. Your activity may be monitored and investigated where inappropriate or unauthorised use is suspected. 

HMPPS can see: 

-   what information you've searched for or viewed 
-   what you've created or changed 
-   when you accessed it 

## If you misuse HMPPS Digital Services 

Unauthorised or inappropriate access, use or sharing of information in HMPPS Digital Services is treated as gross misconduct. It could result in disciplinary action, criminal investigation and prosecution. 

If you suspect someone is misusing HMPPS Digital Services you should report this straight away on the [Counter corruption intranet page](https://justiceuk.sharepoint.com/sites/HMPPSIntranet/SitePages/Counter-corruption-and-wrongdoing.aspx?web=1&or=WORD-WEB.BODY.NT&ct=1791455110846&ct=1791896424600) or telephone 0800 917 6877.
