Using HMPPS Digital Services professionally

Keep records accurate and use HMPPS Digital Services professionally

What you record is used across HMPPS to make operational decisions. Inaccurate entries can lead to poor decisions.

You must:

  • record information accurately
  • update records promptly
  • correct mistakes where appropriate
  • only use HMPPS Digital Services for official HMPPS business

All data you enter into HMPPS Digital Services is potentially liable to legal disclosure under the Data Protection Act and/or the Freedom of Information Act.

Remember your activity is recorded and may be monitored

HMPPS Digital Services keeps an audit trail of your activity. Your activity may be monitored and investigated where inappropriate or unauthorised use is suspected.

HMPPS can see:

  • what information you've searched for or viewed
  • what you've created or changed
  • when you accessed it

If you misuse HMPPS Digital Services

Unauthorised or inappropriate access, use or sharing of information in HMPPS Digital Services is treated as gross misconduct. It could result in disciplinary action, criminal investigation and prosecution.
