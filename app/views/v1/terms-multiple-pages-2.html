Terms and conditions: part 2 of 5

Accessing information responsibly

Only access information you need for your job

Only search, access, view, use or share prisoner information when you have a legitimate work-related reason to do so.

Having access to a prisoner’s profile does not mean you are authorised to view it.

Accessing information without a legitimate work-related reason is unauthorised access.

Anything you do in HMPPS Digital Services must comply with the Computer Misuse Act and other relevant data legislation.

You must not:

  • browse or search records out of curiosity, including friends or family
  • look up prisoners unless you need to as part of your role
  • access records at another establishment or information for someone else, unless you're authorised to do so as part of your work
