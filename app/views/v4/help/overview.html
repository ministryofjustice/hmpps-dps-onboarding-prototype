Locations overview

Use Locations to view and manage residential and non-residential locations, such as:

  • cells and wings
  • workshops, classrooms, healthcare and faith areas

All DPS users can view location information and users with the correct permissions can:

  • manage residential and non-residential location information
  • update the status of cells and other locations
  • submit changes to the cell certificate
  • review and approve certificate changes
  • use accurate location data to support operational decisions
  • maintain an audit trail of updates and approvals
