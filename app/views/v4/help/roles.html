Cell status manager

Wing managers and some prison officers will usually have this role. As a cell status manager, you can:

  • view the status and capacity of locations
  • temporarily deactivate cells that cannot be used
  • reactivate cells when they become available again
  • view the current and previous versions of the cell certificate

Certificate administrator

Heads of residence and some custodial managers will usually have this role.

As a certificate administrator, you can:

  • manage residential location information
  • update capacity, status and location details
  • create new locations
  • submit changes to the cell certificate
  • check the status of submissions
  • view the current and previous versions of the cell certificate

Any change that affects the cell certificate must be approved by your prison group's authorising director before it is applied.

Certificate reviewer

Prison group directors and operational managers in prison group director offices will usually have this role.

As a certificate reviewer, you can:

  • review requests to change the cell certificate
  • assess proposed changes
  • approve or reject requests
  • view the current and previous versions of the cell certificate
